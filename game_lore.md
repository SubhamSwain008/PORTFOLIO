# The Chronicles of the Portals: Shadow & Light

## The Premise & Two Realms
You awaken in a boundless landscape divided into two distinct realities, accessible through mysterious portals. At the heart of your existence stands a monolithic structure of dark charcoal stone—the central building that serves as your anchor in both realms.

### The Night World: "The Hall of Night"
A realm of perpetual twilight and creeping fog. The world is tinted in cold, desaturated colors (cyan and deep blue). Ghostly blue moonlight spills across the landscape. The environment is dense with twisted, skeletal trees and jagged rocks. A dilapidated wooden fence, forming a perimeter 35 units(the fence) from the center, serves as the only boundary between safety and the unknown.
*   **The Atmosphere**: Oppressive, isolating, and eerie. It is a world that drains your vitality. You must manage your Hunger and Stamina; if you sprint too long, you exhaust yourself. If your hunger drops to zero, your health begins to drain until "Starvation has claimed your life."
*   **The Hall of Night**: The central sanctuary. Though its exterior is imposing dark stone, its interior is a haven of warmth. A crackling fireplace casts a deep red, ominous glow. A cozy bed with a draped comforter, a small wooden dining table set with a cup and a book, and mysterious, flickering wall candles provide a stark contrast to the desolate cold outside. It is a place of rest, reflection, and survival.

### The Day World: "The Hall of Light"
Through the portal lies the Day World, a stark contrast to the night. It is bright, bathed in warm sunlight, and the hemisphere light casts a blue sky over green ground. The fog here is light and airy. The structure here is called "The Hall of Light".
*   **The Atmosphere**: Peaceful, explorative, and calm. There are no enemies here. It serves as a reprieve from the relentless pressure of the Night World.

## The Threats: Denizens of the Dark
Beyond the safe zone of the fence in the Night World, the darkness manifests into physical forms. They wander the wastes, drawn to the living. If you venture too far ("I shouldn't go this far from the fence...", "There's nothing out here for me..."), you will encounter them. They cannot breach the fence; the Hall of Night is your only true refuge.

1.  **Skull Specter**: Fragile but incredibly fast (1.5x player speed). A terrifying, floating skull that swarms you quickly.
2.  **Shadow Spider**: Fast and unnerving (1.4x player speed). Skitters through the dark geometry of the world.
3.  **Hellhound**: A demonic canine (1.25x player speed). A balanced hunter of the wastes.
4.  **Banshee Ghost (Floating Ghost)**: A spectral entity that hovers with an eerie presence. Slower (1.1x speed), but packs a localized punch.
5.  **Shadow Wraith**: The most formidable terror. Slow (1.0x speed, matching your walking pace), but boasts the highest vitality and immense damage. A relentless stalker.

When struck, your vision flashes crimson. If they overtake you, the world fades to black: *"An enemy has slain you..."*

## Survival & Sustenance
To survive the Night, you must scavenge the world for sustenance and tools, managing your dwindling hunger.
*   **Mystic Apple**: A shimmering apple infused with arcane energy (Restores vitality).
*   **Golden Bread**: Warm bread baked with enchanted flour (Satisfies deep hunger).
*   **Shadow Mushroom**: A bioluminescent mushroom from the dark forest floor.
*   **Ember Berry**: Tiny berries that glow like embers, warm to the touch.
*   **Moon Cheese**: Pale cheese that glows faintly under moonlight.
*   **Health Potion**: A shimmering crimson elixir.
*   **Healing Herb**: A luminous green herb.
*   **Antidote Vial**: A potent restorative brew.

**Tools of the Explorer:**
*   **Crystal Pickaxe**: Crystalline head that cuts through any stone.
*   **Mystic Torch**: An ever-burning torch that wards off shadows.
*   **Ancient Compass**: A bronze compass that always points toward hidden treasures.

**The Hearth:**
Inside the Hall of Night, the fireplace is not just for warmth. You can cook the raw ingredients found in the wastes:
*   *Roasted Apple*: A warm, caramelized mystic apple.
*   *Mushroom Skewer*: Savory roasted shadow mushrooms.
*   *Moonberry Jam*: A rich, glowing jam made from Ember Berries and Moon Cheese.

## The Experience
The game is a solitary journey of survival, resource management, and exploration. You are tethered to the Hall of Night—your only sanctuary in a hostile, shadowy world. Every excursion past the wooden gates into the fog is a calculated risk against starvation and the terrors that lurk in the dark. The juxtaposition of the warm, crackling fireplace inside against the cold, howling emptiness outside defines the emotional core of the experience. It is a story of enduring the night, managing your fading strength, and occasionally seeking the bright, enemy-free solace of the Hall of Light.
