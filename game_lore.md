# The Chronicles of the Portals: Shadow & Light

## The Premise & Two Realms
You awaken in a boundless landscape divided into two distinct realities, accessible through mysterious portals. At the heart of your existence stands a monolithic structure of dark charcoal stone—the central building that serves as your anchor in both realms. 

### The Night World: "The Hall of Night"
A realm of perpetual twilight and creeping fog. The world is tinted in cold, desaturated colors (cyan and deep blue). Ghostly blue moonlight spills across the landscape, which is dense with twisted, skeletal trees and jagged rocks. A dilapidated wooden fence, forming a perimeter 35 units from the center, serves as the only boundary between safety and the unknown.
*   **The Atmosphere**: Oppressive, isolating, and eerie. It is a world that drains your vitality. You must carefully manage your Hunger and Stamina.
*   **The Hall of Night**: The central sanctuary. Though its exterior is imposing dark stone, its interior is a haven of warmth. A crackling fireplace casts a deep red glow, and a cozy bed offers a place to save your progress.

### The Day World: "The Hall of Light"
Through the portal lies the Day World, a stark contrast to the night. It is bright, bathed in warm sunlight, and a hemisphere light casts a blue sky over lush green ground. The fog here is light and airy.
*   **The Atmosphere**: Peaceful, explorative, and calm. There are no enemies here. It serves as a reprieve from the relentless pressure of the Night World, offering a safer environment to gather resources (though items spawn more sparsely than in the Night World).

## Player Mechanics & Survival Systems
Surviving the world requires careful management of your physical needs:

*   **Stamina**: Your energy for sprinting. You can sprint at up to double your normal walking speed, but doing so drains your stamina rapidly. It takes 30 seconds of rest to fully recover your stamina from zero. Sprinting too much will leave you exhausted and vulnerable if you need to escape.
*   **Hunger**: Your nourishment gradually depletes over a 15-minute cycle. However, if your hunger drops critically low (below 5%), your stamina becomes completely locked, leaving you unable to sprint. If hunger reaches zero, you will slowly starve, draining your health until "Starvation has claimed your life."
*   **Health**: You start with 100 Health. This is depleted by enemy attacks or starvation. Health can only be recovered by consuming medicinal items.

## The Threats: Denizens of the Dark
Beyond the safe zone of the fence in the Night World, the darkness manifests into physical forms. They wander the wastes, drawn to the living. They cannot breach the fence; the Hall of Night is your only true refuge. Up to two of these terrors will hunt you concurrently. 

1.  **Skull Specter**: Fragile (30 HP) but incredibly fast (1.5x player speed). A terrifying, floating skull that swarms you quickly but deals the lowest damage (6 DMG).
2.  **Shadow Spider**: Fast and unnerving (40 HP, 1.4x speed, 8 DMG). Skitters through the dark geometry of the world.
3.  **Hellhound**: A demonic canine (60 HP, 1.25x speed, 12 DMG). A balanced hunter of the wastes.
4.  **Banshee Ghost (Floating Ghost)**: A spectral entity that hovers with an eerie presence. Slower (80 HP, 1.1x speed), but packs a localized punch (15 DMG).
5.  **Shadow Wraith**: The most formidable terror. Slow (1.0x speed, matching your walking pace), but boasts the highest vitality (120 HP) and immense damage (20 DMG). A relentless stalker, but it has a crucial weakness: **it freezes completely when caught in your flashlight's beam**.

When struck, your vision flashes crimson. If your health reaches zero, the world fades to black: *"An enemy has slain you..."*

## Scavenging, Sustenance & Cooking
To survive the Night, you must scavenge the world for sustenance and tools. Items can be found scattered across the world, indicated by glowing orbs from a distance.

**Raw Ingredients:**
*   **Mystic Apple**: A shimmering apple infused with arcane energy.
*   **Golden Bread**: Warm bread baked with enchanted flour.
*   **Shadow Mushroom**: A bioluminescent mushroom from the dark forest floor.
*   **Ember Berry**: Tiny berries that glow like embers, warm to the touch.
*   **Moon Cheese**: Pale cheese that glows faintly under moonlight.

**Medicine:**
*   **Healing Herb**: A luminous green herb. (Restores 15 Health)
*   **Health Potion**: A shimmering crimson elixir. (Restores 30 Health)
*   **Antidote Vial**: A potent restorative brew. (Restores 50 Health)

**The Hearth & Cooking:**
Inside the Hall of Night, you can use the fireplace to cook raw ingredients into potent meals that restore your Hunger. Cooking takes varying amounts of time depending on the dish:
*   *Roasted Apple* (Requires 1 Mystic Apple): A warm, caramelized mystic apple. Restores 15 Hunger.
*   *Mushroom Skewer* (Requires 2 Shadow Mushrooms): Savory roasted shadow mushrooms. Restores 25 Hunger.
*   *Moonberry Jam* (Requires 2 Ember Berries, 1 Moon Cheese): A rich, glowing jam. Restores 40 Hunger.

## Tools of the Explorer
*   **Flashlight**: Your primary tool for navigating the dark. Beyond visibility, its beam is crucial for freezing the deadly Shadow Wraiths in their tracks if they get within 25 units.
*   **Mystic Torch**: An ever-burning torch that wards off shadows. When equipped, it creates a 5-unit radius safe zone around you, actively repelling enemies for 60 seconds before burning out.
*   **Crystal Pickaxe**: Crystalline head that cuts through any stone.
*   **Ancient Compass**: A bronze compass that always points toward hidden treasures.

## The Experience
The game is a solitary journey of survival, resource management, and exploration. You are tethered to the Hall of Night—your only sanctuary in a hostile, shadowy world. Every excursion past the wooden gates into the fog is a calculated risk against starvation and the terrors that lurk in the dark. The juxtaposition of the warm, crackling fireplace inside against the cold, howling emptiness outside defines the emotional core of the experience. It is a story of enduring the night, managing your fading strength, using light to your advantage, and occasionally seeking the bright, enemy-free solace of the Hall of Light.
